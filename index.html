Context

Born and raised in Sydney, I started in IT back when Unix shells were all the rage. I then spent 13 years in Europe, swapping code for colour theory and studying Fine Arts at the University of Valencia.

Now I work in Strategic Product, where logic, empathy and chaos intersect. I help design products that make sense… and make people smile. Off-screen, you'll find me at the piano, cooking paellas, or turning “short” trips into decade-long adventures.

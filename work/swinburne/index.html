Work / Swinburne University

The Swinburne student experience

Due to COVID-19, Swinburne’s students needed a platform to collaborate and stay informed. Over three months, our team of nine released an MVP that went on to win international awards.

Role
UX Design Specialist
Company

  1. Problem
  2. Solution

Problem

For many years the Swinburne app had gone unused, for a variety of reasons: outdated design and information, poor usability, and errors accessing content.

Student feedback suggested that adding single sign-on to all services within the app would greatly improve the experience, and customer support told us which features received the most enquiries.

Users’ unmet needs

Students needed to stay up to date with Canvas, the learning management system, and to download class material from the library while the physical library was closed. They also wanted reminders about upcoming classes, as managing their own time while studying from home was hard.

Business needs

The university needed a communication channel to inform students of regulation changes due to COVID, and a way to provide mental health, financial and academic support.

Solution

Gathering data from the call centre, we noticed students were asking for six tools, which we prioritised as features for the first release.

Personalisation has become an integral part of designing a digital system, so we added a hello message to the MVP to build a connection with each student.

Production

To create a winning experience, we designed a prioritised feature set and workflow, overlaid with analytics success and failure metrics to optimise future releases.

We created a design language system for efficiency, consistency and scale. It was based on the global brand guidelines and adapted for the best viewing on mobile devices.

Outcomes

After the initial soft launch, and compared with the previous year, we saw an exponential increase in daily usage and unique visitors with each new release.

The app was nominated by Modo Labs to compete in the worldwide Appademy Awards for 2020, and Swinburne received Best Overall Design. This was our MVP!
